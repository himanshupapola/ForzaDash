Forza Data Out telemetry generally gives your car/player telemetry only, not a full lobby roster.

What this app/server is already parsing includes:

racePosition: your current race position
lapNumber: your current lap number
currentLap: current lap time
lastLap: last lap time
bestLap: best lap time
currentRaceTime: total/current race time
positionX/Y/Z: your car world position
speed, RPM, gear, throttle, brake, tire temps, suspension, etc.
What it does not normally give:

All player names
Other players’ positions
Current player/gamertag name
Full race leaderboard names
So: yes, it can show your position and timing data, but no, it cannot show all player names or your gamertag/player name from standard Forza telemetry alone.

Race complete percent could be estimated from lapNumber plus track distance if enough distance/lap data is reliable, but it may be approximate.
